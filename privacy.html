KC Browser Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KC Browser handles information when you use the Android application.

1. Information stored on your device

KC Browser stores browsing history and bookmarks locally on your device so you can access and manage them. Browsing history from incognito sessions is not added to the normal browsing history.

2. Website information

When you visit a website, that website may receive information such as your IP address, browser information and information associated with its cookies or other technologies. Each website has its own privacy practices, which KC Browser does not control.

3. Incognito browsing

Incognito browsing uses a separate browsing profile. It is designed to keep that session separate from normal browsing history. It does not make you anonymous online. Websites, internet service providers and network administrators may still be able to observe your activity.

4. Downloads

When you download a file using KC Browser, Android's download service manages the download and saves the file to your device's Downloads location.

5. Accounts and cloud storage

KC Browser does not require you to create an app account. The app's browsing history and bookmarks are stored locally rather than being uploaded to a KC Browser cloud account.

6. Your choices

You can manage your browsing history and bookmarks through the browser's available controls. You can also manage downloaded files through your Android device.

7. Children's privacy

KC Browser is a general-purpose browser and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application or its features change. The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For questions about this policy, please use the KC Browser website repository issue page .
